Logo concepts

Five ideas for the symbol, each two strokes in two colours. Left: on the brand blue. Right: on paper in the two seat colours. Below: 32 px, 16 px and next to the name. Drawn for this project, not traced from anything.

Relay

Penduet

One line, two hands. Started by one, carried on by the other, no seam. The simplest of the five; strongest at 16 px.

Two ends

Penduet

A wave with a pen point at each end. Reads as two people on one line. The dots are the most readable thing at tiny sizes.

Co-sun

Penduet

The first lesson-style picture: one draws the circle, the other the rays. Friendliest, and it matches the screenshot on the home page. Sun icons are common, so the two colours do the work.

Braid

Penduet

Two lines that start apart, cross in the middle and go on. Looks most like a mark. Check it does not read as a DNA or recycling symbol next to a tile.

P in two strokes

Penduet

The initial, drawn by two people. Ties the symbol to the name, so it has to be redrawn if the name changes.
