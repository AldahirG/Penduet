# Brand: Penduet

Written 2026-10-27 by `/replica-brand`. The angle comes from `fixes.md`: **draw with someone you know, with no bots, no ads and no energy meter.**

The name is a recommendation that has not been cleared. The checks below are screening checks, run on the date shown, and several are still "to run". Before money goes into the name (a domain, a logo, a store listing), a trademark lawyer should search it properly.

## The name

**Penduet** (pen + duet: two people, one canvas). It is the working name now, used in the app, the packages and the docs. Renaming again later is a mechanical change.

Twenty candidates were considered across styles, then cut:

| style | candidates |
| --- | --- |
| descriptive | Sketchmate, Doodlepair, Pairsketch, Tracemate, Pencilmate |
| compound | Twoline, Pairpen, Cocanvas, Inkpal, Marginmate, Sideline, Chalkpal, Pairpaper, Twinline |
| invented | Scribbo, Linka, Penna, Doodlebud |
| metaphor | Sidewalk (chalk on a pavement), Scrawl |

Cut at once because an app with that name already exists in the App Store search (run 2026-10-27): Sketchmate (a drawing game), CoCanvas, Scribbo (a draw-and-guess game), Doodlebud, Scrawl, Sidewalk, TwoLine and others, and every one of the first 20 has its `.com` already registered. A second round of 50 invented names was screened on the same two tests: 14 had a free `.com`, and 13 of those also had no app of that exact name.

`Pairdoodle` and `Squigglepair` were cut after a web search found **PairDrawing**, a real-time two-person drawing app. Anything with "Pair" next to "draw" sits too close to it, in sound and in meaning. Note that PairDrawing is a direct competitor in this exact niche; see `fixes.md` before launch.

Not confusingly close to the original (ArtWorkout): no "Art", no "Workout", no shared sound. Not close to Duolingo (a "duo" name would be): "duet" is a different word, but it is the one to watch.

Trademark, Google Play and social handles were **not** run for any of them.

**Clear on all four screens** (nothing registered, no app that starts with the name, no GitHub user):

| **Pencolab** | pen + collaboration | clear, but "colab" is a generic word in software |
| **Duetpen** | duet + pen | the same two words as Penduet in the other order; too close to be a real alternative |

Taken on at least one screen, so cut: Fourhands, Crosshatch, Hatchmate, Duoink, Sidelines, Inkling, Inklet, Tracepal, Handinhand, Coscribe, Marginalia, Cohatch, Inkmeet, Inkshare, Twinline (an app of that name exists), and many others whose `.com` was registered. Two apps in the same niche turned up while checking: **CoSketch** ("Drawing Together") and **PairDrawing**; stay away from names near them.

The pick, if it were mine: keep **Penduet** unless the "-uet" spelling bothers you in testing (people mishear it as "Penduette"), and otherwise **Tandoodle** or **Tandempen** as the alternatives. Say a name aloud to someone who has never seen it, and ask them to type it: that is the test that matters.

## Palette

Replaces the placeholder (warm paper and indigo) in `replica/design/tokens.json` and `tokens.dark.json`. Same role names; only values changed. Regenerated into the app with `pnpm tokens`.

- **Brand hue: cobalt ink-blue**, `#2b4fd8` on light, `#6a88ee` on dark. Neutrals go from warm to cool paper-white and blue-grey.
- **Why it is different from the original.** I looked at the original's public home page (colours read from the page, nothing copied): near-black text `#252222` on a cream page `#fdf8f2`, with pale blue `#e9f3ff` / `#cfe3fc` and pink `#ffdbe9` panels, and green progress marks in its app screenshots. The new palette shares none of those: cool white page, blue-grey text, cobalt accent. The drawing paper stays near-white (`#fdfcf9`) because strokes must look the same to both people in any theme.
- **Seat colours** (who drew what): orange-red `#c2410c` and teal `#0b7285`, unchanged. Both pass 3:1 on the paper.
- Those six original colours are in `brand.json`, so the sweep catches any that creep back.

Contrast (`python replica/design/contrast.py`): light 25 pairs, 0 failing AA. Dark 25 pairs, 0 failing AA. The dark accent was tuned to pass on both the dark page (5.6:1) and the light drawing paper (3.2:1), because it is used for focus rings on both.

## Logo brief

Not a logo. A brief for whoever draws it (you, a designer, or an image model).

- **The idea:** two lines that start apart and finish as one drawing. Two people, one canvas.
- **Mark type:** symbol plus wordmark. The symbol must also work alone.
- **Must work** at 16 px (favicon) and as a 1024 px app icon.
- **Deliverables:** SVG master; app icon 1024 x 1024 with no transparency (iOS); favicon set (SVG, 32, 180); social image 1200 x 630; one-colour and white-on-colour versions.
- **Stand-in in the app today:** `apps/web/public/favicon.svg`, a cobalt rounded square with two overlapping strokes (white and warm yellow). It is a placeholder, drawn for this project, not a finished mark.
- **Must not resemble the original's mark.** Their app icon is a rounded square showing a painted portrait, and their wordmark is a plain bold sans. Put it next to any draft: no shared shape, no portrait, no cream-and-black pairing.
- **Avoid** pen-nib clichés shared with every drawing app, and anything that spells a "D" from two halves (too close to the duet apps in the category).

## Voice

Three words:

- **Friendly, not chatty.** It talks to you like a person next to you, in a sentence or two.
- **Plain, not babyish.** Short words, no jargon, and it is fine for adults and children to read the same line.
- **Honest, not gloomy.** It says what happened and what to do next, with no blame and no fake cheer.

Do and don't:

| do | don't |
| --- | --- |
| "Open a room, send the code, and draw on one canvas." | "Unleash your creativity with real-time collaborative canvases!" |
| "No open room has that code. Check it with your partner." | "Error 404: room not found." |
| "Lost the connection. Trying to get you back in the room…" | "Oops! Something went wrong." |
| "Nothing drawn yet. Trace the dashed guide, then check again." | "Invalid submission." |
| "The other person is always a real person." | "Join millions of artists worldwide!" |

Rules: say what happened, then what to do. Never blame the person. No exclamation marks outside a real celebration. Never promise something the product cannot keep: "free" and "no ads" are written only while they are true.

## The sweep

`python sweep.py . --config replica/brand.json` finds nothing of the original's name, domains or colours in the code, config and tests (the planning folder `replica/` is skipped on purpose: it records the recon). The first run reported 1,050 hits, all in generated build caches (`.wrangler/`), which hold the absolute path of the folder this project lives in. Those caches were deleted and the sweep ran clean.

By eye: the page title, the favicon and the theme colour were updated. There is no email template, no OG image and no app icon yet (they come with the logo).

**One thing the sweep cannot fix: the project folder on disk is still called `ArtWorkout`.** Rename it (for example to `penduet`) before this goes in a repository or on a public host, or its name will appear in paths and commit metadata.
